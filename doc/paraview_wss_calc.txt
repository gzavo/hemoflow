Wall shear stress (WSS) from HemoFlow output in ParaView
=========================================================

1. Write the stress tensor: add to the <simulation> section of the config
       <outputFields>velocity pressure stress</outputFields>
   The field "stress [Pa]" is the viscous (deviatoric) stress tensor in SI units with
   6 components, in the order XX, XY, XZ, YY, YZ, ZZ (ParaView shows them as 0..5).

2. Load the .vti output and the vessel STL. With geometry files from the current
   preprocessor the output carries the physical origin, so it overlays the STL once the
   STL is scaled from mm to m (Transform filter, scale 0.001).

3. Compute surface normals on the STL (Generate Surface Normals) and make sure they
   point out of the lumen (flip them otherwise).

4. Sample the stress on the surface (Resample With Dataset; source: the .vti,
   destination: the STL). Wall voxels carry zero stress, so values sampled exactly on
   the wall are underestimated. Sample on a copy of the surface moved about one voxel
   (dx) into the lumen instead (Warp By Vector with the normals scaled by -dx).

5. Traction t = sigma . n (Calculator, result "traction"):

   iHat*("stress [Pa]_0"*Normals_X + "stress [Pa]_1"*Normals_Y + "stress [Pa]_2"*Normals_Z) +
   jHat*("stress [Pa]_1"*Normals_X + "stress [Pa]_3"*Normals_Y + "stress [Pa]_4"*Normals_Z) +
   kHat*("stress [Pa]_2"*Normals_X + "stress [Pa]_4"*Normals_Y + "stress [Pa]_5"*Normals_Z)

6. WSS vector = tangential part of the traction (Calculator, result "WSS"):

   traction - dot(traction, Normals)*Normals

   The magnitude of "WSS" is the wall shear stress [Pa]. Time-averaged WSS (TAWSS) and the
   oscillatory shear index (OSI) follow from the WSS vectors of the last cycle.
